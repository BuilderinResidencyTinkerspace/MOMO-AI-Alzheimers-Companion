Component Research
Objective

Identify and research the hardware components required to build MOMO.

Activities

●	Researched compute options, settling on the Raspberry Pi 5 as the core processing unit

●	Identified peripheral components: microphone, speakers, and sensors

●	Explored options for a 3D-printed enclosure/box for the tabletop form factor

Outcome

A component list was finalised, covering the Raspberry Pi 5, microphone, speakers, sensors, and 3D-printed enclosure, forming the basis for the build phase.
